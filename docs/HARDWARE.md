# Hardware shopping list

Physical things the mission will need, to be dealt with when the software needs them. The agent
keeps this list current and must not buy anything, which is just as well.

Readiness: `exists`, `prototype`, `lab`, `paper` or `fantasy`. Needed: `now`, `soon`, `someday`.

| Item | Why | Readiness | Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sail film, under 1 g/m² for the whole craft, survives 850 K | Everything depends on this | lab | someday |
| Deployment booms, kilometre scale | Unfolding a sail that size without tearing it | prototype | someday |
| Launch to a sundive orbit | Getting close to the Sun means shedding all of Earth's orbital speed | exists | someday |
| Star tracker, milligram scale | Navigation from star positions | lab | someday |
| Interstellar radio or laser transmitter | Phoning home across 4.34 light-years | paper | someday |
| Long-life power source, thousands of years | Sunlight disappears a few weeks after launch | paper | someday |
| Ground receiver, kilometre-scale optical array | Hearing the reply | paper | someday |
| Sail handling robot | Assembling a fragile mirror in orbit | prototype | someday |
| Small vacuum chamber and a lamp | Measuring sail-film heating ourselves, as a cheap first experiment. The best dive now depends entirely on the film's reflectivity and emissivity, which are assumed, not measured: going from 0.9 to 0.95 reflectivity moves the dive about 30% closer to the Sun | exists | soon |
| A second Sun, for testing | Would make the environmental tests much easier | fantasy | someday |
